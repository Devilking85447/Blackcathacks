What is Dark Web? Truth, Myths and Safety Guide 2025

Published by BlackCatHacks | Deep Dive

Introduction

Everyone talks about the Dark Web, but what is it really? Is it illegal to visit? Many YouTubers show scary stories about it. Today we will clear all myths and tell you the real truth in simple language.

Understanding the Internet - 3 Layers

Internet has 3 parts:

  1. Surface Web: The normal internet you use daily - Google, YouTube, Instagram. Only 4% of the internet.
  2. Deep Web: The private part - Your Gmail inbox, bank account, company database. Google cannot search it. It's 90% of the internet and is totally legal.
  3. Dark Web: A small part of Deep Web (around 6%) which is intentionally hidden. You need a special browser called Tor Browser to access it.

Is Dark Web Illegal?

Visiting the Dark Web is NOT illegal in India. Tor Browser is 100% legal. The technology itself is legal. But many illegal activities happen there, so you should be very careful. Just like a knife is legal, but using it for crime is illegal.

What is Inside the Dark Web?

Dark Web is used by two types of people:

Good Use: Journalists, whistleblowers, and people in countries where internet is censored use it to communicate safely and privately.

Bad Use: Unfortunately, some people use it for illegal marketplaces, which is why it has a bad reputation. Law enforcement agencies like FBI and CBI constantly monitor it.

Should You Visit the Dark Web?

For a normal student or beginner, NO. There is no need to visit it out of curiosity. It is risky. Your device can get viruses and your privacy can be at risk. If you are a cybersecurity student, only visit it in a safe virtual machine for educational research.

How to Stay Safe on the Internet?

  • Never download files from unknown sources.
  • Always use a good VPN and Antivirus.
  • Never share your personal documents on unknown websites.
  • Use strong and different passwords for every account.

Conclusion

Dark Web is not a scary horror movie. It is just a technology for privacy. But like every technology, some people misuse it. As a responsible internet user, you should focus on securing your own Surface Web life first. That is more important than exploring the Dark Web.
