How to Secure Your WiFi From Hackers - 7 Easy Tips (2025)

Published by BlackCatHacks | Security Guide

Introduction

Is your WiFi slow? Someone might be using your WiFi without your permission. In India, WiFi hacking is very common. But don't worry, you can secure your WiFi in just 5 minutes by following these 7 simple tips.

1. Change Your Default Router Password

Most people never change the admin password of their router. The default password is usually "admin" or "password". Any person can guess it. Go to 192.168.1.1 in your browser and change your admin password to a strong one with numbers and symbols.

2. Use WPA3 or WPA2 Security

Always check your WiFi security type. Never use WEP or Open security. WEP is very old and can be hacked in 2 minutes. Always use WPA2 or the latest WPA3 security. You can change this from your router settings.

3. Create a Strong WiFi Password

Don't use your name, mobile number, or 12345678 as your password. A strong password should have at least 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Example: MyWiFi@2025#Secure

4. Hide Your WiFi Name (SSID)

You can hide your WiFi name so that other people cannot see it when they search for WiFi. Only people who know the exact name can connect. This option is called "Hide SSID" in router settings.

5. Enable MAC Address Filtering

Every device has a unique MAC address. You can allow only your family's devices in your router. Go to MAC Filtering option and add your mobile and laptop MAC addresses. Now no other new device can connect, even if they know your password.

6. Keep Your Router Firmware Updated

Router companies release updates to fix security problems. Most people never update their router. Go to your router's admin panel and check for firmware update. An updated router is much safer.

7. Turn Off WPS

WPS is a button on your router to connect quickly without a password. But it is very insecure. Hackers can easily hack WiFi using the WPS feature. Always disable WPS from your router settings.

Conclusion

Your WiFi is the gateway to all your devices. If your WiFi is not secure, all your mobiles, laptops, and bank accounts are at risk. Follow these 7 tips today and make your WiFi 100% secure. For more such ethical hacking and security tips, stay connected with BlackCatHacks.
