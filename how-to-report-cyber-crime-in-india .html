How to Report Cyber Crime in India? Step-by-Step Guide (1930 Helpline)

Published by BlackCatHacks | India Guide

Got Scammed Online? Do This in First 10 Minutes

In India, thousands of people lose money daily to online fraud. Most people don't report because they don't know how. If you report within 2 hours (Golden Hours), there is 80% chance to get your money back.

Step 1: Call 1930 Immediately

1930 is the National Cyber Crime Helpline by Government of India. It works 24x7. Call them and tell your full story - when money was deducted, how much, from which UPI or bank. They will freeze the scammer's account immediately.

Step 2: File Complaint on cybercrime.gov.in

  1. Go to cybercrime.gov.in
  2. Click on "Report Cyber Crime"
  3. Select "Report Financial Fraud" if money is involved.
  4. Fill details - your name, bank details, screenshot of transaction, scammer's number/UPI ID.
  5. You will get an Acknowledgement Number. Save it.

Step 3: Go to Your Nearest Cyber Cell

If amount is big (more than Rs 50,000), go to your district's Cyber Crime Police Station with printout of complaint, bank statement, and ID proof. They are very helpful now.

What Details to Keep Ready?

  • Screenshot of payment / UPI transaction
  • Scammer's mobile number, UPI ID, or website link
  • Your bank account number and transaction ID
  • Time and date of fraud

What Happens After Reporting?

After you report on 1930, the system sends alert to all banks. Scammer's account gets lien marked (frozen). If money is still in his account, it comes back to you in 7-15 working days after police verification.

Important Tips

Never delete chat with scammer - it is proof. Never pay anyone who says "Give me Rs 2000 and I will return your money". No police asks for money. Only use official website cybercrime.gov.in.
