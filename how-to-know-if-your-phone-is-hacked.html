How to Know If Your Phone is Hacked? 10 Signs You Should Not Ignore

Published by BlackCatHacks | Mobile Security

Is Your Phone Hacked?

Your phone has everything - your bank, your photos, your chats. If it gets hacked, your whole life is at risk. But how do you know if your phone is hacked? Here are 10 clear signs.

10 Signs Your Phone is Hacked

  1. Battery Drains Very Fast: Your battery finishes in 2-3 hours even without using. It means some hidden malware is running in background.
  2. Phone Becomes Very Hot: Even when you are not using it, your phone is hot. Malware is using your processor.
  3. Unknown Apps Installed: You see apps you never installed. Delete them immediately.
  4. Data Usage is Very High: Your 1.5GB daily data finishes in 1 hour. Hacker is sending your data to his server.
  5. Strange Pop-ups and Ads: You see ads even on home screen or in settings. This is adware.
  6. Messages Sent Without You Knowing: Your friends say "Why are you sending strange links?" You didn't send them - hacker did.
  7. Phone is Very Slow: Even after clearing RAM, phone lags a lot.
  8. Camera or Mic Turns On Automatically: You see green dot on top of your Android phone even when you are not using camera.
  9. OTP Not Coming: You are not receiving OTPs because your SMS are being forwarded to hacker.
  10. Bank Money Deducted: The worst sign. Small amounts like Rs 1, Rs 10 deducted first to test.

What to Do Immediately If You Think You Are Hacked?

  • 1. Turn on Flight Mode.
  • 2. Uninstall all unknown apps from Settings > Apps.
  • 3. Run a scan with Play Protect or Malwarebytes app.
  • 4. Change all your passwords from another device (not from hacked phone).
  • 5. If problem still exists, take backup and do Factory Reset.

How to Stay Safe in Future?

Never install APK files from Telegram or WhatsApp. Only install from Play Store. Don't click on links saying "You won iPhone" or "See your leaked video". And keep your phone updated - every update fixes security bugs.
