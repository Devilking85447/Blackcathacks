What is Phishing Attack? How Hackers Steal Your Bank Account in 1 Click

Published by BlackCatHacks | Must Read for Everyone

What is Phishing?

Phishing is the number 1 fraud in India. In this, a hacker sends you a fake message or email that looks 100% real - like from SBI Bank, Flipkart, or even your friend. When you click on that link and enter your details, your information goes directly to the hacker.

Real Example in India

You get an SMS: "Your SBI account is blocked. Click here to do KYC: sbi-kyc-update.com" The website looks exactly like SBI. You enter your username, password, OTP. Within 2 minutes, your account becomes empty. This is phishing.

Types of Phishing You Must Know

  1. SMS Phishing (Smishing): Fake SMS about lottery, job, bank KYC.
  2. Email Phishing: Fake emails saying "Your Gmail storage is full, click here".
  3. Voice Phishing (Vishing): A call from someone saying "I am calling from bank, tell me your OTP". Bank never asks for OTP.
  4. Social Media Phishing: Fake Instagram message "I saw your photo on this site, is this you?" with a link.

How to Identify a Fake Link?

  • Check spelling: Real is sbi.co.in, fake is sbi-kyc.com or sbionline.xyz
  • Check for https and lock icon, but remember - even fake sites can have https now.
  • Don't click in hurry. If message says "Urgent! Do now or account will be closed" - it's 99% phishing.
  • Hover your mouse on link (on PC) to see real link before clicking.

How to Protect Yourself?

  1. Never share OTP, CVV, or password with anyone - not even bank staff.
  2. Never click on links from unknown numbers.
  3. If you get a bank message, open the official app directly, don't click the link in SMS.
  4. Turn on 2FA on all your accounts.
  5. If you clicked by mistake, immediately change your password and call your bank to block card.

What to Do If You Got Scammed?

Immediately call 1930 - This is the National Cyber Crime Helpline in India. Also report on cybercrime.gov.in. The faster you report (within 2 hours), the higher chance to get your money back.
