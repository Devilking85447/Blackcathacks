How to Secure Your Instagram Account From Hackers (2025 Guide)

Published by BlackCatHacks | Social Media Safety

How Instagram Accounts Get Hacked?

Every day 1000+ Instagram accounts get hacked in India. Hackers send fake messages like "Your account will be blue-ticked, click here" or "Someone tried to login, verify here". When you click, they steal your password. Sometimes they use leaked passwords from other websites.

5 Steps to Secure Your Instagram 100%

Step 1: Turn On Two-Factor Authentication (Most Important)

Go to Settings > Accounts Centre > Password and Security > Two-factor authentication. Choose Authentication App (Google Authenticator) - it is safer than SMS. Now even if hacker has your password, he cannot login.

Step 2: Check Login Activity

Go to Settings > Password and Security > Where you're logged in. If you see any unknown device or city, click "Log out" and immediately change your password.

Step 3: Remove Third-Party Apps

Many people use "Who viewed my profile" or "Free followers" apps. These apps steal your password. Go to Settings > Website Permissions > Apps and Websites and remove all suspicious apps.

Step 4: Create a Strong Password

Don't use your name or birth year. Use 12+ characters with symbols. Example: Insta$afe#2025@MyAcc. And never use same password for Gmail and Instagram.

Step 5: Beware of Phishing DMs

Instagram will NEVER DM you. If you get any DM saying "Copyright violation" or "Verify account", it is 100% fake. Don't click any link.

What to Do If Your Instagram Gets Hacked?

  1. Immediately go to instagram.com/hacked and report.
  2. Check your email - Instagram sends a mail "Your email was changed" - click "Secure your account here".
  3. Try to reset password using your phone number.
  4. If nothing works, report with a video selfie - Instagram will return your account in 24-48 hours.

Conclusion

Your Instagram is your digital identity. Just 2 minutes of security setup can save you from years of regret. Turn on 2FA today and remove unknown apps. Stay safe.
