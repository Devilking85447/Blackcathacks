How to Create a Strong Password That No One Can Hack (2025 Guide)

Published by BlackCatHacks | Security Guide

Why 80% Passwords Get Hacked?

Most people use passwords like 123456, password, or their name + birth year. Hackers can crack these in less than 1 second using tools. If you are using the same password everywhere, you are in big danger.

What Makes a Password Strong?

A strong password has 4 things:

  • 1. At least 12 characters long
  • 2. Mix of Capital + Small letters (A, a)
  • 3. Numbers (0-9)
  • 4. Special Symbols (@, #, $, %)

Example of a WEAK password: Rohit123

Example of a STRONG password: R0h!t@My$ecure#2025

3 Tricks to Create and Remember Strong Passwords

Trick 1: Sentence Method

Take a sentence you will never forget. Example: "I love to eat Momos in Delhi at 9 PM"
Now take first letter of each word: IlteMiDa9PM -> Now add symbols: IlteMiDa@9PM! -> This is a super strong password and easy to remember.

Trick 2: Use a Password Manager

Don't remember passwords in your mind or in notes. Use trusted password managers like Bitwarden (Free) or 1Password. They create and save strong passwords for you. You just need to remember one master password.

Trick 3: Different Password for Every Website

This is the golden rule. Never use same password for Gmail, Instagram and Bank. If one site gets hacked, all your accounts will be hacked. Password manager will help you in this.

What is Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?

Even with a strong password, always turn on 2FA. It means even if someone knows your password, they cannot login without a code from your mobile. Turn it on for your Gmail, Instagram, Facebook today itself. It is 99% protection.

Final Checklist

  • Check if your password is leaked: Go to haveibeenpwned.com and check your email.
  • Change your password every 3 months.
  • Never share your password with anyone.
