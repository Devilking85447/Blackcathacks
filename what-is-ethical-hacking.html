What is Ethical Hacking?

Ethical Hacking is when someone hacks a system with permission to find security problems and fix them. The person who does this is called an Ethical Hacker or White Hat Hacker. Their job is to protect systems, not to break them. For example, a company will say "Please try to hack our website and tell us where it is weak" - and the ethical hacker does that legally.

Ethical Hacker vs Cracker - What is the Difference?

Many people get confused. Let me clear it:

  • Ethical Hacker (White Hat): Hacks with permission, to protect people. This is legal and a high-paying job.
  • Cracker (Black Hat): Hacks without permission, to steal data and money. This is illegal and you can go to jail.

Types of Ethical Hacking

  1. Web Application Hacking: Finding bugs in websites like Flipkart, Facebook. Companies pay lakhs for this.
  2. Network Hacking: Testing how secure a company's WiFi and network is.
  3. System Hacking: Testing how secure Windows, Linux computers are.
  4. Mobile Hacking: Testing Android and iOS apps for security flaws.

Skills Required to Become an Ethical Hacker

You don't need to be a genius. You need these 5 skills:

  • 1. Basic knowledge of Computer Networking
  • 2. Knowledge of Linux (Kali Linux)
  • 3. Basic programming - Python is best for beginners
  • 4. Problem-solving mindset
  • 5. Most Important - Patience and continuous learning

Ethical Hacking Career and Salary in India

This is one of the most demanding careers in 2025.

  • Beginner (0-2 years): ₹4 - 8 Lakhs per year
  • Mid-Level (2-5 years): ₹10 - 20 Lakhs per year
  • Expert / Bug Bounty Hunter: ₹30 Lakhs+ and some people earn crores by finding bugs for Google and Facebook.

Companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Google, and Microsoft are always hiring ethical hackers.

How to Start Learning Ethical Hacking for FREE?

Step 1: Learn Networking basics from YouTube (search - Network Fundamentals).
Step 2: Install VirtualBox and Kali Linux on your laptop.
Step 3: Start practicing on legal websites like TryHackMe.com and HackTheBox.com.
Step 4: Never practice on any real website or WiFi without permission.

Conclusion

Ethical Hacking is not about doing illegal things. It is about making the internet a safer place. If you are interested in computers and security, this can be the best career for you. It has high salary, respect, and you get to work with top companies.
